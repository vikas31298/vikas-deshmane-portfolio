Business context

The problem

As co-founder, the brief was to build a discover-to-purchase-to-serve journey from scratch that could hold up under exam-season spikes, while using AI to evaluate learner responses at scale with high relevance.

My role

Scope and ownership

Owned end-to-end product strategy, PRDs and roadmap. Ran prioritisation from funnel dashboards and experiments, and partnered with engineering on API design, retry handling and failure monitoring for AI pipelines.

Product strategy

How I approached it

  1. Instrumented the full funnel from day one so friction points were found with data rather than opinion.
  2. Chose a microservices stack (Angular, FastAPI, PostgreSQL) with CI/CD and horizontal scaling to survive traffic spikes.
  3. Made AI evaluation a core product capability with explicit relevance targets and failure monitoring.
  4. Ran continuous experiments on the purchase and serve steps to lift completion and cut support contacts.

Tech architecture

How the platform is put together

An Angular front end talks to FastAPI microservices over PostgreSQL. Orders, payments and content delivery are separate services with retry handling. AI evaluation pipelines process learner submissions asynchronously with relevance monitoring and fallbacks.

Request lifecycle

How one learner moves through the platform

  1. Discover: a learner finds a course through catalogue and search.
  2. Purchase: orders and payments complete in a simplified checkout.
  3. Learn: content delivery serves lessons and practice tests.
  4. Submit: answers go to a queue instead of blocking the learner.
  5. Evaluate: AI workers score submissions asynchronously, with retries and fallbacks.
  6. Feedback: results return to the learner with relevance-checked explanations.
  7. Improve: funnel dashboards and experiments decide the next round of changes.
