Overview

Business context

Small businesses in India struggled to get credit because they lacked collateral and formal financial statements. After GST launched, their filed returns became a verified record of sales and purchases, but no lender had yet built a credit product on that data.

Responsibility

My role

Led the program from the CEO's office: defined the product, the GST data integration, the underwriting rules with risk, and the digital journey, working with partners who brought small-business customers.

Approach

Product strategy

  1. Use GST returns as the primary underwriting signal for turnover, stability and growth.
  2. Get the business owner's consent to fetch GST data directly, avoiding manual document uploads.
  3. Encode risk policy in the configurable rule engine so thresholds could change without code.
  4. Distribute through partners who already served small businesses, onboarding them by configuration.

Judgement

Key decisions

  • Built on GST data rather than bank statements alone, because filed returns were harder to manipulate.
  • Required explicit consent for every data fetch to keep the program compliant from day one.
  • Started with conservative limits and widened them as repayment data came in.

System architecture

End-to-end architecture

With the business owner's consent, GST return data is fetched and turned into cash-flow features. Risk models and the configurable rule engine decide, and approved loans are booked on the loan management system.

Request lifecycle

Request lifecycle

  1. Apply: a small business applies through a partner or directly.
  2. Consent: the owner authorises access to their GST returns.
  3. Fetch: GST, bank and bureau data are pulled automatically.
  4. Analyse: returns are turned into turnover, consistency and growth features.
  5. Decide: risk models and the rule engine set approval and limit.
  6. Disburse: sanction, e-sign and mandate happen digitally before disbursal.
  7. Monitor: repayment and fresh GST filings feed portfolio monitoring.
