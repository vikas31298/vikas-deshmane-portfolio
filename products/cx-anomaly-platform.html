Business context

The problem

A multi-entity, multi-brand real-estate group collected millions of customer reviews and survey responses across projects, cities and brands. Insight was retrospective: failure themes surfaced only once they had become complaints, escalations and NPS damage. Operations teams had no early signal and executives had no single view across entities.

My role

Scope and ownership

Owned product vision, strategy and roadmap for the platform end to end. Translated CXO goals into measurable outcomes, ran executive updates and trade-off discussions, and partnered with engineering, architecture, data engineering and analytics across orchestration, data platform and dashboard workstreams.

Product strategy

How I approached it

  1. Reframed the problem from reporting to prevention: detect emerging failure themes and route them to the owning operations team before they become complaints.
  2. Chose an agentic architecture (LangGraph on Azure OpenAI) so analysis, classification and routing could run continuously rather than as monthly reports.
  3. Treated accuracy as a release-quality control: a 165-question evaluation harness gated at 80%+ accuracy decides whether a version ships.
  4. Designed a hybrid curated-SQL and constrained text-to-SQL path so executives get reliable answers to known questions and safe answers to new ones.

Tech architecture

How the platform is put together

Event-driven ingestion feeds a governed data platform built around a 466-entity hierarchy with time-varying weights. LLM agents classify and detect anomalies, a routing layer assigns owners, and a query layer serves dashboards and conversational analytics with retry and fallback at every hop.

Key decisions

Trade-offs I would defend

  • Chose constrained text-to-SQL with a curated fallback over open-ended generation, trading flexibility for trust with executive users.
  • Invested early in an evaluation harness rather than shipping fast and fixing later; this became the shared release criterion with engineering.
  • Modelled time-varying entity weights so historical comparisons stayed valid as the group restructured.
