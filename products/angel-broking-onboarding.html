Overview

Business context

Angel Broking was growing fast as new investors entered the market. Opening a trading and demat account needed identity, address, bank and income documents, and manual verification took around two days. Many customers dropped off before their account was active.

Responsibility

My role

From the COO's office, redesigned the account-opening journey and led the automation of KYC: document classification, entity extraction and fraud checks, working with operations, compliance and data science.

Approach

Product strategy

  1. Treat time-to-first-trade as the main metric, not just form completion.
  2. Automate document checks with BERT-based classification and entity extraction.
  3. Run fraud and consistency checks automatically and send only exceptions to people.
  4. Keep a full audit trail so compliance could approve the automation.

Technology

Architecture

Customers complete a digital journey and upload documents. Document AI classifies each file and extracts key fields, which are cross-checked against each other and against fraud rules. Clean applications activate automatically and exceptions go to reviewers.

Judgement

Key decisions

  • Measured success by time-to-activation, which exposed verification as the real bottleneck.
  • Kept humans on low-confidence documents rather than forcing full automation.
  • Designed the audit trail first so compliance sign-off did not block launch.

AI architecture

End-to-end architecture

A digital journey collects details and documents, document AI classifies and reads every file, automated checks verify and screen for fraud, and clean applications activate without manual review.

Request lifecycle

Request lifecycle

  1. Sign up: the investor starts on mobile and enters personal and bank details.
  2. Upload: identity, address and bank documents are captured in the app.
  3. Check quality: blurry or duplicate images are flagged immediately so the user can retake them.
  4. Read: document AI classifies each file and extracts names, numbers and dates.
  5. Verify: extracted fields are matched across documents and screened by fraud rules.
  6. Review: only low-confidence or flagged cases go to a reviewer.
  7. Activate: trading and demat accounts open and the investor can place a first trade.
