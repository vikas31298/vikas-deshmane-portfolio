Overview

Business context

Lending growth was capped by manual credit evaluation and a 60-day partner onboarding cycle. Each new B2B partner required bespoke integration work, and servicing requests from 5M customers landed on human agents.

Responsibility

My role

Owned the loan management system of record from the CEO's office. Defined the rule engine, led lending digitisation with ML-based instant decisioning, and delivered an NLP self-service chatbot for customer servicing.

Approach

Product strategy

  1. Made credit policy configuration a product capability: a rule engine for validation, scoring and approval that risk teams could change without code.
  2. Standardised partner integration so onboarding became a configuration exercise rather than a project.
  3. Introduced ML-based instant decisioning for straightforward cases and reserved human review for exceptions.
  4. Automated the highest-volume servicing requests through an NLP chatbot with intent classification and entity extraction.

Technology

Architecture

A loan management system of record sits at the centre, with a configurable rule engine for validation, credit evaluation, risk scoring and approval. Partner APIs feed applications in, ML decisioning scores them, and a servicing layer with an NLP chatbot handles customer requests.

Judgement

Key decisions

  • Built the rule engine to be owned by risk, not engineering, so policy changes shipped in hours.
  • Automated the easy majority first and kept humans on exceptions, which raised capacity without raising risk.
  • Prioritised partner onboarding as a growth lever because distribution, not credit appetite, was the constraint.

System architecture

End-to-end architecture

A loan management system of record with a configurable rule engine at its core. Partners integrate through standard APIs, ML scores routine cases instantly, and an NLP assistant handles servicing.

Request lifecycle

Request lifecycle

  1. Onboard: a new partner is configured, not coded, which cut onboarding from 60 days to 2.
  2. Apply: applications arrive through standard partner APIs with documents attached.
  3. Validate: the rule engine checks eligibility and completeness using partner-specific rules.
  4. Score: risk scoring and ML decisioning approve routine cases instantly.
  5. Review: exceptions route to credit analysts with the reasons attached.
  6. Book: approved loans are written to the system of record with a full audit trail.
  7. Service: the NLP chatbot resolves common requests and routes the rest, cutting manual support by 60%.
