Business context

The problem

Sales teams and channel partners sold many loan products, each with its own eligibility rules, pricing and document checklist. Product information sat in long PDFs and circulars that changed often, so reps called product teams, gave customers wrong answers, or lost deals while waiting.

My role

Scope and ownership

Owned the product end to end: knowledge sources, conversation design, eligibility tools, compliance guardrails and rollout to sales teams.

Product strategy

How I approached it

  1. Answer only from approved, versioned product content so reps never quote outdated terms.
  2. Pair retrieval with deterministic tools for eligibility and EMI calculations instead of letting the model do maths.
  3. Design for the field: short answers, document checklists and shareable summaries for customers.
  4. Log every unanswered question to show product teams where content was missing.

Key decisions

Trade-offs I would defend

  • Used tools for eligibility and EMI maths because model arithmetic is not reliable enough for pricing.
  • Restricted answers to approved content even when it meant replying 'I don't know'.
  • Launched with one product line first, then expanded once accuracy was proven.

Agentic AI architecture

Request lifecycle

How one sales question is answered

  1. Ask: a rep asks a question about a loan product during a customer conversation.
  2. Route: the orchestrator identifies the product and the type of question.
  3. Retrieve: the knowledge agent pulls the relevant approved content.
  4. Calculate: eligibility and EMI questions go to deterministic tools.
  5. Compose: the answer is written briefly with the document checklist where relevant.
  6. Check: the compliance checker blocks guarantees and outdated terms.
  7. Learn: unanswered questions are logged for product teams to fill.
