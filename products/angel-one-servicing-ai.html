Business context

The problem

Support for 10M+ brokerage customers across email and chat was manual and slow to route, producing repeat contacts and long resolution times. KYC document checks for onboarding and fraud verification took days.

My role

Scope and ownership

From the COO's office, defined and delivered the NLP-powered CRM and automated KYC programs, working across operations, support, compliance and data science.

Product strategy

How I approached it

  1. Targeted failed and repeat interactions first: classify every incoming query and route it to the right resolver on the first attempt.
  2. Used transformer models for query classification so routing quality improved with volume instead of degrading.
  3. Applied BERT-based document classification and entity extraction to KYC so verification became minutes instead of days.
  4. Measured success by contact rate and resolution time rather than tickets closed.

Tech architecture

How the platform is put together

Incoming email and chat flow through an NLP classification layer that assigns intent and routes to queues or automated resolvers. A separate document pipeline classifies uploads, extracts entities and runs fraud checks before KYC approval.

Key decisions

Trade-offs I would defend

  • Started with routing rather than full automation because misrouting was the biggest driver of repeat contacts.
  • Kept humans in the loop for low-confidence classifications and measured confidence thresholds explicitly.
  • Built the KYC pipeline with a full audit trail so compliance could sign off on automation.
