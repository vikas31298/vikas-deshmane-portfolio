Business context

The problem

LLM features were reaching production without systematic checks for accuracy, hallucination or safety, and inference spend was growing with volume. Finance and procurement teams also needed fast, compliant answers on SLAs, renewals and contract terms buried in documents.

My role

Scope and ownership

Led technology, product and business as head of function. Owned the evaluation platform, the RAG assistant and the data-warehouse ETL framework, including cost and telemetry efficiency programs.

Product strategy

How I approached it

  1. Put automated quality gates (accuracy, hallucination detection, safety) in the release path so quality became measurable and enforceable.
  2. Ran a cost-optimisation program on inference and telemetry rather than accepting spend as proportional to volume.
  3. Delivered the RAG assistant with guardrails, content filtering and role-based access so it could pass financial compliance review.
  4. Rebuilt ETL on change-data-capture with validation checkpoints to cut cost and improve freshness.

Tech architecture

How the platform is put together

An evaluation and monitoring layer wraps every LLM feature with offline test suites and online quality gates. The RAG assistant runs on Azure OpenAI and Cognitive Search with guardrails and RBAC. A CDC-based ETL framework with validation checkpoints feeds the enterprise data warehouse.

Key decisions

Trade-offs I would defend

  • Treated hallucination and safety as release-blocking defects, equal to functional bugs.
  • Chose managed Azure services to meet compliance and RBAC requirements quickly rather than building custom infrastructure.
  • Used CDC instead of batch extracts so the warehouse stayed fresh without full reloads.
