About

A leader who connects business outcomes, platform strategy and execution.

I have led product and technology for business-critical systems of record: the order, credit, payment and servicing platforms every other team depends on. I set the vision and roadmap, align executives on trade-offs, and build the cross-functional teams that deliver, from the CEO's office at a lender to co-founding and scaling a platform of my own.

How I work

The operating principles behind the numbers.

01

Start from the failed interaction

Every roadmap item traces to a measurable customer or operator outcome: success rate, contact rate, time-to-resolution, cost per transaction.

02

Design for the unhappy path

Retry, fallback, idempotency and routing are product decisions, not just engineering ones. I sit in the design reviews.

03

Gate releases with evidence

Evaluation harnesses, quality gates and experiments decide what ships. Opinions get a vote, data gets the veto.

04

Influence without authority

15+ cross-functional teams, CXO stakeholders, finance and risk. Clear trade-offs, honest updates, shared scorecards.
