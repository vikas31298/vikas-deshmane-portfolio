About

About

I have led product and technology for business-critical systems of record: the order, credit, payment and servicing platforms every other team depends on. I set the vision and roadmap, align executives on trade-offs, and build the cross-functional teams that deliver, from the CEO's office at a lender to co-founding and scaling a platform of my own.

Over the last few years I've applied AI/ML and LLM agents to the reliability problem itself: anomaly detection that flags failure themes before they become complaints, automated quality gates that catch defects pre-deployment, and agent-assisted operations that cut contact rates. Everything is instrumented. I run on dashboards, experiments and root-cause analysis.

How I work

How I work

01

Start from the failed interaction

Every roadmap item is tied to a measurable customer or operational outcome, such as success rate, contact rate or cost per transaction.

02

Design for the unhappy path

Failure handling, retries and routing are product decisions as much as engineering ones. I take part in design reviews.

03

Gate releases with evidence

Evaluation suites, quality gates and controlled experiments decide what ships.

04

Influence without authority

I have coordinated more than fifteen cross-functional teams and worked directly with CEOs, COOs, finance and risk leaders through clear trade-offs and shared scorecards.
